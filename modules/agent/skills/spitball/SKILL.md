# Spitball

Its time to spitball!
Brainstorm with the user, throw ideas out there, see what sticks.
The goal of spitballing is to figure out what we might've missed, not thought about yet, or forgotten to think about.

## How to spitball

- Make a list: Line-up all candidates we mightve not thought about, a naive person would expect, or an expert engineer would've spot.
- Check it twice: Compare, Constrain, Contrast, Prioritize
- Find out what's naughty or nice: Judge, find the winners
- Go to town on it: Present it to the user, build visuals if needed
